Privacy policy

Time Tracker for Autodesk Fusion

HomePrivacy policy

Time Tracker runs entirely on your computer. It does not collect, transmit, share or sell any personal data. It makes no network connections and has no accounts or analytics.

What it stores, only on your PC: the names and identifiers of the designs you work on, daily work durations (total and mouse-moving), manual time entries you type, the names of your computers, and your settings. This lives in a small database in the FusionTimeTracker folder under your local application data. Deleting that folder removes everything the add-in has recorded.

What it reads: the time of your last keyboard or mouse input, which window is in front, and whether the mouse pointer has moved since the previous second. It never reads or stores keystrokes, typed values, pointer coordinates, screenshots, or the content of any other application.

What leaves your PC: when you save a design, the add-in stores its time totals for that design as a small text attribute inside the design. Autodesk Fusion then syncs the design, including that note, under Autodesk's own terms and privacy policy. Nothing is sent by the add-in itself.

Changes to this policy will be published with new versions of the add-in.

HOW TO PUT THIS SITE ONLINE (free, about ten minutes)

Option A - GitHub Pages
1. Sign in at github.com and create a new public repository named  fusiontimer
2. Upload every file in this folder (index.html, privacy.html, logo.png, time-tracker-icon.png).
3. In the repository: Settings > Pages > Source: "Deploy from a branch", Branch: main, folder: / (root). Save.
4. After a minute the site is at   https://YOURNAME.github.io/fusiontimer/
   Company URL       = https://YOURNAME.github.io/fusiontimer/
   Privacy Policy URL = https://YOURNAME.github.io/fusiontimer/privacy.html

Option B - Netlify Drop
1. Go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag this whole folder onto the page.
2. It gives you an address like https://something.netlify.app ; privacy.html is at /privacy.html.

Before uploading: open index.html in Notepad and replace "(your support email)" with the address
you want customers to use, and change the App Store button link once your listing is live.
